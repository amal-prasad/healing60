## 🧠 Design Concept: "The Inner Space"

The core idea is to make the visitor feel like they're gently traveling **inward** — from the outside world into a calm, safe mental space. Here's how each section could feel:

**Hero Section**
A looping 3D scene — imagine soft, slow-moving organic blobs or a floating brain-like abstract form rendered in muted lavender, sage green, and warm cream. As you scroll, it slowly transforms or "breathes." A headline like *"A space where healing begins."* The 3D object reacts subtly to mouse movement on desktop, and gyroscope tilt on mobile.

**About / Philosophy Section**
As you scroll, text fades in with a parallax depth effect — words appearing to float at different Z-depths. A soft particle field drifts in the background, giving the impression of thoughts floating freely. Very calm, no hard edges.

**Services Section**
Cards that have a gentle 3D card-flip or tilt-on-hover effect (using CSS perspective). Each service (anxiety, couples therapy, etc.) gets a minimal 3D icon — think Spline-designed shapes that feel organic and human.

**Meet the Therapists**
Clean portrait cards with a glassmorphism treatment — frosted glass panels that shift slightly on hover. Think Superpower's clean-but-warm photo treatment.

**Testimonials**
Horizontal scroll carousel with subtle depth layering — front cards sharp, background cards blurred, giving a sense of 3D space.

**Booking / CTA**
A full-screen atmospheric closing section — warm gradient background (like a sunset or soft auroral glow), a calming 3D animated form or just a bold, centered CTA. Something that feels like "arriving."

## 📱 Mobile Experience

The animations don't die on mobile — they *adapt*:
- 3D scenes simplify (lower polygon count via LOD — level of detail)
- Scroll-triggered animations remain but are less GPU-intensive
- Gyroscope-driven tilt replaces mouse parallax
- Spline scenes can be swapped for pre-rendered video loops as fallback
- Glassmorphism cards and soft gradients render beautifully on OLED mobile screens

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Framework: Next.js 14 (App Router)**
Gives you server-side rendering, fast page loads, and great SEO — crucial for a clinic that needs to be found locally on Google.

**Styling: Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion**
Tailwind handles layout and responsive design. Framer Motion handles scroll-linked animations, fade-ins, and entrance effects with near-zero config. It's mobile-aware out of the box.

**3D Layer: Spline + Three.js (React Three Fiber)**
Spline for designer-built 3D scenes that export directly to React with one `<Spline>` component. React Three Fiber (R3F) + Drei for custom 3D interactions like the particle field, morphing blobs, or depth-layered scenes. Spline scenes are surprisingly mobile-optimized.

**Scroll Engine: GSAP ScrollTrigger**
The gold standard for scroll-linked animation timelines. Syncs 3D scene progress, text reveals, and section transitions to scroll position — exactly what Superpower.com uses.

**CMS: Sanity.io**
So the clinic staff can update therapist bios, blog posts, and services without touching code.

**Deployment: Vercel**
Zero-config, globally fast CDN, perfect with Next.js.

**Booking Integration: Cal.com (open source) or Calendly embed**
Styled to match your design system rather than looking like a foreign widget.

**Performance Safety Net: @react-three/drei's `<Preload>` + Suspense**
3D assets load progressively, so mobile users on slower connections still see a beautiful experience — just without the heaviest effects until ready.

## 🎨 Palette & Vibe

Steal from the psychology of color: **lavender** (calm, trust), **warm off-white** (safety), **sage green** (growth, healing), with **deep charcoal** for text — never pure black. The 3D objects should feel like they're made of soft frosted glass or milky translucent resin.
